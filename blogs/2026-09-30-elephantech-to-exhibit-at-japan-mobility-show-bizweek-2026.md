Elephantech will exhibit at Japan Mobility Show Bizweek 2026, taking place at Makuhari Messe, Japan, from October 13 to 16, 2026. Organized by the Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA), the event serves as a high-level industry platform to bring together automakers, startups, universities and research institutions, and companies. Through exhibits and a variety of programs, the event offers opportunities for co-creation across industries and fields.
