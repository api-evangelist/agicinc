On Wednesday, October 7, 2026, Shinya Shimizu, CEO of Elephantech, will speak at “Impact Challenge of the year 2026,” a pitch event presented by Forbes JAPAN. “Impact Challenge of the year 2026” is a pitch event where impact startups balancing social impact with business growth share the challenges they are taking on and the potential of their businesses. First held in 2025, the event returns for its second edition this year.
